Content Advisor - Detail Panel Customization (infoPopoverMap)

Open either selector below, then use an asset's info action to open the detail panel. The first tab (Info) is rendered from detailPanelInfoPopoverMap in detail-panel.js.

Requires advisor mode and an apiKey that Adobe has enabled for this capability. See docs/content-advisor-detail-panel-customization.md.

Runtime apiKey: (not provided). Supply it via ?apiKey=<your-key>; no key is hardcoded or persisted by this example.

The filter panel is customized too (filterSchema, filterSchemaSource and filterSchemaDefaults in filter-schema.js). It opens pre-filtered to images. Filter panel customization is not limited to enabled apiKeys.
